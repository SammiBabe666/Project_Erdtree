erdtree for the Hundekraft Workshop: an Elden Ring-inspired synth engine for the original OP-1 (firmware 246)
Blue choir, green strings, white space, orange brass, with an animated Erdtree on T1.

This zip is for the Hundekraft Workshop 3.0.x only. For Opus 2, download erdtree-opus.zip instead: its plugin and
its presets do not work on the Workshop, and these do not work on Opus.

THE ENGINE  (Hundekraft Workshop/erdtree.knl, version 0.5)
  1. Open the Workshop (https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) with an OP-1 that runs Workshop 3.0.
  2. Add erdtree.knl under "Your plugins", press "Update OP-1", read the review, send, and press T1 on the OP-1.
  3. After the restart, open synth, press Shift + T1 and choose erdtree.

THE PRESETS  (presets/synth/erdtree, 8 sounds)
  Install erdtree first. In disk mode (Shift + COM, then T3), copy the "erdtree" folder from presets/synth into the
  OP-1 disk's synth/ folder, so the files end up in synth/erdtree/ (for example synth/erdtree/first grace.aif). Eject;
  the OP-1 syncs them and waits for a key press. To play them: in synth mode hold Shift and press a sound key, turn
  blue to the erdtree folder and green to the preset. The OP-1 keeps 300 user synth presets in all; past that the
  sync drops the extra ones.

THE ENCODERS
  Blue    choir    spectral voices with a slow breath and shimmer       leaves fall from the crown
  Green   strings  detuned violins and celli, bowed swell, vibrato      the crown grows and sways
  White   space    distance: wider, darker, slower, deeper breath       more stars, twinkling
  Orange  brass    the Brassophone horn, brighter with more brass       shafts of light break through
  Blue, green and orange are section levels: all three at zero is silence. White is not a reverb; put one on the
  synth's effect slot for a hall. T2 (envelope), T3 (effect) and T4 (LFO) are the OS's own.
