erdtree for Opus 2: a dark-fantasy orchestra engine for the original OP-1 (firmware 246) running Opus 2
Blue choir, green strings, white space, orange brass, with an animated tree on T1.

This zip is for Opus 2 only. For the Hundekraft Workshop, download erdtree-hundekraft.zip instead: its plugin and
its presets do not work on Opus, and these do not work on the Workshop.

THE ENGINE  (Opus 2/erdtree.opmod, version 0.6.1, needs Opus core 2.8 or later)
  1. Disk mode: press Shift + COM, then T3, and connect the OP-1.
  2. Copy erdtree.opmod into the disk's "mods" folder. Eject, then switch the OP-1 off and on.
  3. Opus's menu (hold synth + drum + tape + mixer), then T2 for the mods list: erdtree's row says "loaded".
  4. In synth mode press Shift + T1 and choose erdtree.
  Its setting "screen" (the mods list, blue push on erdtree's row): animated, or still to draw the picture only when
  a knob moves, which uses less CPU.
  If erdtree is silent: switch developer mode on (Opus's menu, T1) and play again. If it sounds then, Opus was
  switching it off for going over its CPU figure: send the "audio: worst N" from its details (orange push on its row).
  If anything goes wrong, hold Stop while switching the OP-1 on (no plugins load), then delete the file.

THE PRESETS  (presets/synth/erdtree, eight sounds made for Opus)
  Install the engine first. In disk mode, copy the "erdtree" folder from presets/synth into the OP-1 disk's synth/
  folder, so the files end up in synth/erdtree/ (for example synth/erdtree/crown of ash.aif). If that folder holds
  erdtree's older Hundekraft presets (first grace, golden order and so on), delete those first: they do not load on
  Opus. Eject; the OP-1 syncs the files and waits for a key press. To play them: in synth mode hold Shift and press a
  sound key, turn blue to the erdtree folder and green to the preset.

  crown of ash         majestic, warm brass over strings, solemn
  the hollow belfry    struck brass and choir ringing out with no sustain, far away (bell-like)
  thornwake            sharp, dry, short bowed strings with a brass bite
  vesperglass          the choir's harmonics alone, an octave up, shimmering
  the sunken choir     the full choir, far and dark, breathing, strings beneath
  grave of giants      brass near full an octave down, celli under it
  moonless vigil       quiet on purpose: soft, distant strings and a breath of choir
  the unwritten god    choir and brass at once, as far as it goes, an octave down, swelling for seconds

THE ENCODERS
  Blue    choir    spectral voices with a slow breath and shimmer       leaves fall from the crown
  Green   strings  detuned violins and celli, bowed swell, vibrato      the crown grows and sways
  White   space    distance: wider, darker, slower, deeper breath       more stars, twinkling
  Orange  brass    the Brassophone horn, brighter with more brass       shafts of light break through
  Blue, green and orange are section levels: all three at zero is silence. White is not a reverb; put one on the
  synth's effect slot for a hall. T2 (envelope), T3 (effect) and T4 (LFO) are the OS's own.
