## 🌳 erdtree: an Elden Ring orchestra synth for the OP-1

A new synth engine for the original OP-1, as a `.knl` plugin for the **Hundekraft Workshop 3.0**, with 8 presets.

You conduct a little orchestra under the Erdtree. Each knob brings in its own section:
- 🟠 **Orange**: brass (the Brassophone horn, brighter as it gets louder)
- 🟢 **Green**: strings (detuned violins and celli, bowed swell, late vibrato)
- 🔵 **Blue**: choir (breathing "aah" voices with a shimmer)
- ⚪ **White**: space (wider, darker, slower to bloom)

The screen is the Erdtree over Leyndell in OP-1 line style. Brass breaks light through the crown, strings sway it, the choir drops golden leaves and space lights up the stars.

**Presets:** first grace, golden order, malenia lament, erdtree dreams, starscourge, leyndell hall, roundtable, royal stab. Stock effects only.

**Status (v0.5):** runs on the OP-1 with clean chords, about 38 % audio load while playing. Headphones and the effects on each preset aren't tested yet, so feedback is welcome.

The video is rendered from the plugin code on a computer (reverb added), not recorded from the OP-1.

**Install:** add `erdtree.knl` under *Your plugins* in the Workshop, press *Update OP-1* and confirm with T1 on the OP-1. After the restart, pick **erdtree** in synth mode with Shift + T1. For the presets, copy the `erdtree` folder into `synth/` in disk mode.
